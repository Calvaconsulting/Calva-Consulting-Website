01 Who I am

The thread isn't advocacy. It's operating discipline.

I'm Michael Ruiz. In thirty years of executive leadership, I've directed $4.2 billion in economic activity to underserved businesses, turned a financially distressed nonprofit to sustained profitability, and co-founded an industry coalition that reshaped diversity practice across fifty mortgage firms.

Every meaningful advance in economic equity I've been part of happened because someone engineered it — into procurement strategy, board composition, and revenue structure. Through Calva Consulting, I advise organizations at the intersection of economic equity, supplier diversity, and community development, translating mission into an operating model that holds under board scrutiny, regulatory examination, and financial pressure at the same time.
